How CrowdFund Works

Start raising funds in minutes. No complicated process

01

Create Your Campaign

Sign up and create a campaign by adding a title, description, goal amount, and campaign image. Build your story and share why your cause matters to inspire donors.

02

Share With Your Community (Share & Promote)

Share your campaign with your family and friends and social media to spread awareness and gather support. Engage your audience, track analytics, and grow your community of supporters.

03

Recieve Donations / Get Funded

As donations come in, watch your campaign grow and get funded. Withdraw your funds easily and securely. Use the funds to bring your project to life and make a difference in the world. Receive donations, update supporters, and bring your vision to life successfully.

04

Withdraw Funds

Once your campaign is complete, withdraw your funds easily and securely. Manage your finances and ensure transparency with your supporters.
